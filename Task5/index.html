Stories of Change — Grassroots Chronicles

The Reading Circle at Doddaballapur

In February 2025, InAmigos volunteers arrived at a semi-rural government school with 120 storybooks and art supplies. What began as a 1-hour weekend workshop evolved into a thriving weekly literacy circle. Today, over 80 young students have improved their reading comprehension, and five older students have trained to lead peer reading sessions.

From Concrete Patch to Green Haven

An abandoned civic plot in the East Ward was repurposed into a native butterfly and shade sanctuary. Over 45 neighborhood families contributed weekend sweat equity to plant 200 saplings, construct natural composting pits, and nurture green biodiversity.
